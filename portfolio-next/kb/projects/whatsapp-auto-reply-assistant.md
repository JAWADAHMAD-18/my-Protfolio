## Overview
A personal WhatsApp bot that replies in the owner's own texting style. It routes contacts by tier, transcribes voice notes, and uses a self-approval flow for sensitive topics. Code: https://github.com/JAWADAHMAD-18/whatsapp_bot

## Stack
Baileys, Groq, Groq Whisper, MongoDB, Node.js.
